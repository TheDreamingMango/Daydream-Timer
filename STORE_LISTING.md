## Full description

Maladaptive daydreaming is not ordinary mind-wandering. It can involve prolonged, compulsive absorption in vivid fantasies—sometimes for hours at a time. It can interfere with work, study, sleep, relationships, and everyday responsibilities.

Daydream Timer is built specifically for people who experience maladaptive daydreaming. It makes elapsed time harder to lose track of while you are absorbed in a daydream.

Start the timer with one tap. Every minute, it speaks the elapsed time aloud. At regular intervals, it also reads a brief grounding quote. These spoken interruptions provide an external cue to notice how long you have been away and choose whether to return to the present.

The timer keeps running when your screen is locked or another app is open. Because music and other audio can intensify or sustain maladaptive daydreaming, Daydream Timer briefly interrupts that audio for each announcement and restores it immediately afterward.

This is a focused tool for a serious and often misunderstood problem—not a meditation course, habit tracker, or productivity game.

- One-tap start and stop
- Spoken minute announcements
- Brief grounding quotes
- Works in the background
- No account required
- No ads
- No internet connection required
- Your sessions stay on your device

Daydream Timer is a grounding and time-awareness tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition and is not a substitute for qualified professional care.
